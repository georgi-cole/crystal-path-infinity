Owner-requested menu follow-up, 5 November 2026, PR #83.

Upper-left Settings remains with the quick audio toggles. The bottom
navigation is Rankings, Profile, Store. Store is a distinct `#store` route
with normal Back/deep-link behavior and the continuous menu soundtrack.

The owner selected six catalog categories: SP, hints, no ads, bridge designs,
backgrounds and themes. The obsidian catalog presents Essentials and Make it
yours filters, with native keyboard tab navigation and original SVG/CSS
illustrations. It is a preview and clearly says purchases are unavailable.

cosmetic ownership are fabricated. Product SKUs, pricing, authoritative grants,
no-ads behavior and platform billing still need a separate implementation.
