Reproducibility and limitations
Same seeds across combinations; independent bot choice stream. Current game reducer, mystery weights, cooldowns and progressive clock. Human learning, voluntary quitting, retention and sales are not modelled. The resource-aware bot uses its observed break frequency, hints, public omens and balances, never the private tile assignments.
