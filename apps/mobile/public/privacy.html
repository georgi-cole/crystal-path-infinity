Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy covers Crystal Path: Infinity on the web and in the mobile app. The public web game offers guest play. The sections on accounts, purchases and rewards apply when those features are available in the version you use.

Who we are

Innovation Creativity Opportunities Ltd, registered in Sofia, Bulgaria under UIC 208316618, is the controller responsible for player data. For privacy questions and requests, email support@kolequant.xyz. Company information

What we use and why

Guest play and site delivery

Guest play requires no account, email address or age answer. The game keeps preferences, tutorial progress, saved crossings and local scores in your browser or app storage. The web host receives connection information and logs visitor IP addresses for security. This helps deliver the game and protect the service. GitHub Pages data collection

Account eligibility and sign-in

When you request an account, we check the age and country of residence you provide alongside the country of your connection. We use the exact age to make that decision. The game stores the eligibility category and country for eligible requests. The gateway uses a protected identifier derived from the connection address to limit abuse. If you are eligible and proceed, we use your email to send a one-time code and to create or recover your account after verification.

If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, that provider authenticates you. We verify its account identifier and verified email to create or reconnect your game account. A name supplied by the provider may appear privately in your profile. Existing accounts require proof of ownership before linking. Apple credentials needed for revocation are encrypted on our server and used when deleting the account.

We use account identifiers, profile choices, sessions and online gameplay records to provide account features you request. If you participate in a public ranking, it may display your chosen player name, score and ranking region. An optional age group answer in your profile is used for analytics if you choose to provide it. It is separate from account eligibility.

Provider sign-in attempts may briefly store encrypted responses while returning you to the app. Attempts expire after five minutes and are removed during scheduled cleanup. The app return link contains no provider credentials or game session.

Purchases and rewarded ads

When native purchases are available, Google Play or Apple handles payment. We use product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and entitlements to deliver, restore and reconcile purchases and process refunds. The tester web store uses simulated items and keeps them in that browser.

When rewarded ads are available, Google’s advertising and consent services can process network and device information, privacy choices and ad interactions. We use signed reward confirmations to credit the game reward. Where applicable, use the app’s privacy options to change your choices.

Support and legal bases

If you email us, we use your address and the details you provide to answer your request. Where the GDPR applies, we use data as needed to provide the account or purchase you request, to pursue our legitimate interests in delivering and securing the service and responding to support, and to comply with applicable legal obligations. We rely on consent for optional analytics and where required for advertising. You can withdraw consent for future processing.

Providers and international transfers

GitHub Pages hosts the web game. Cloudflare handles connections to the account gateway and forwards support email. Supabase hosts the account database in Ireland. Resend delivers verification emails and processes their delivery details. Apple and Google provide the native stores, and Google provides advertising and consent services when enabled. Our mailbox provider processes support messages.

Providers may process information in other countries. Where data protection law requires safeguards for a transfer, we use an applicable adequacy decision or contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about the safeguards that apply to your data.

Retention and deletion

Local guest data stays on your device until you clear it. Verification codes expire within ten minutes. The service removes the delivery payload after a successful send and clears expired challenges during maintenance. We retain your account and online records while needed to provide the account. We keep support correspondence and security records as needed to resolve requests, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations.

Deleting an account removes its profile, sessions and associated game data from active systems. Limited transaction records may be retained to meet accounting obligations, resolve refunds or prevent duplicate delivery. Backup copies may remain until the backup cycle expires. You can request account deletion even if you cannot sign in.

Children, choices and your rights

Account availability depends on the age and residence rules that apply to the player. Where an account requires age assurance or a parent or guardian’s permission, registration stays closed until the required checks are available. Younger players can use guest play. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions or removal requests.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or a review of an account eligibility decision. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent where it applies. Email support@kolequant.xyz to make a request. We may verify that the request concerns your account. You can complain to your local data protection authority or to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.
